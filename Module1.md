## Long Answer Questions (5 Marks)

### #209
* **Question:** Explain the fundamental meaning of a strategic decision in business. In your answer, briefly describe how a long-term strategic decision differs from routine operational choices made by daily branch managers.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * **Definition:** A major, high-stakes choice determining the long-term direction, scope, and resource allocation of an enterprise.
  * **Time Horizon:** Strategic spans 3–5+ years; operational spans day-to-day or weekly cycles.
  * **Scope & Impact:** Strategic cuts across all functional silos; operational is localized to single departments or branches.
  * **Risk & Reversibility:** Strategic decisions entail high capital risk and are costly to reverse; operational adjustments are agile and low risk.

### #210
* **Question:** Explain the key characteristics that define strategic decisions. In your response, outline why strategic decisions involve significant resource commitment, high uncertainty, and organization-wide impact.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * Detail (1) Significant Resource Commitment (capital, talent, technology), (2) Pervasive Uncertainty (dynamic markets, competitor actions), (3) Cross-functional Impact (altering operations, finance, and marketing), and (4) Sunk Cost & Irreversibility.

### #211
* **Question:** Explain the core pillars that support effective strategic decisions in an enterprise. Discuss how organizational vision, internal resources, and external market conditions act together to guide managerial direction.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * **Pillar 1:** Organizational Vision & Mission (defining strategic intent).
  * **Pillar 2:** Internal Resource Base & Capabilities (VRIO framework: tangible and intangible assets).
  * **Pillar 3:** External Market Conditions (PESTEL, Porter's Five Forces).
  * **Synthesis:** Strategy emerges at the intersection of what the firm wants to do (Vision), can do (Resources), and is allowed to do (Environment).

### #212
* **Question:** Explain the primary objectives behind making strategic business decisions. Using a simple retail or service business example, show how strategic choices aim to secure competitive advantage and long-term sustainability.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * Discuss competitive advantage, enterprise longevity, and stakeholder value creation.
  * **Case Example:** A regional supermarket investing in a dark-store / 30-minute quick-commerce delivery model to build customer switching barriers and protect margins against national e-commerce rivals.

### #213
* **Question:** List the four essential characteristics of strategic decisions and state the primary objective of strategic management in an organization.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * **Four Characteristics:** Long time horizon, substantial resource commitment, organization-wide impact, and high uncertainty.
  * **Primary Objective:** Systematically align internal capabilities with external opportunities to achieve sustainable competitive advantage.

### #214
* **Question:** Define the term "strategic decision" and name the key pillars upon which strategic decisions are formulated.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * Define comprehensive forward-looking choices by top management.
  * Enumerate pillars: Strategic Intent/Vision, Internal Competencies, and External Market Environment.

### #215
* **Question:** Analyze how the external business environment influences internal strategic choices. Using a small local business expanding into online delivery, examine the critical internal strengths versus external threats that shape its strategic scope.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * **External Factors:** Digital payment penetration, aggregator fee structures (18–30%), customer convenience trends.
  * **Internal Strengths:** Direct food quality control, hyper-local brand loyalty.
  * **Strategic Synthesis:** Pursue a hybrid model—utilizing aggregators for customer acquisition while promoting direct WhatsApp/app channels for retention.

### #216
* **Question:** Compare and contrast strategic decisions with tactical operational decisions across time horizon, level of risk/resource commitment, and degree of reversibility.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * Contrast along the 3 parameters using a clear table or structured paragraphs (3–10 yrs vs days/weeks; existential capital risk vs minor departmental expense; irreversible sunk costs vs easily modifiable procedures).

### #217
* **Question:** Examine a situation where a smartphone company must decide between entering an emerging market or upgrading its current product line. Analyze how the pillars of strategic decision-making help leadership break down and structure this choice.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * Apply Vision (volume vs premium margin play), Internal Resources (supply chain cost engineering vs high-end R&D talent), and External Dynamics (price-sensitive emerging market growth vs replacement cycle slowdown in mature markets).

### #218
* **Question:** Analyze why strategic decisions carry a high degree of irreversibility. In your answer, examine the structural and financial reasons why reversing a major strategic expansion causes significant loss to a firm.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * **Financial Drivers:** Sunk capital costs, asset specificity (steep fire-sale haircuts), and contractual cancellation penalties.
  * **Structural Drivers:** Organizational inertia, brand dilution/reputational damage, and employee severance liabilities.
