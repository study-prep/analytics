## Long Answer Questions (5 Marks)

### #239
* **Question:** Define Business Analytics and identify the three standard classifications of analytics (Descriptive, Predictive, and Prescriptive).
* **Answer Framework:**
  * **Definition:** Systematic iterative evaluation of data using statistical and computational models to generate actionable business intelligence.
  * **Descriptive Analytics:** Evaluates historical data to answer *"What happened?"* (e.g., revenue summaries, dashboards).
  * **Predictive Analytics:** Applies statistical regression and machine learning to forecast *"What is likely to happen?"* (e.g., customer churn, demand forecasts).
  * **Prescriptive Analytics:** Employs mathematical optimization and simulation algorithms to answer *"What should we do?"* (e.g., algorithmic pricing, automated route optimization).

### #240
* **Question:** List four key organizational benefits of adopting data-driven decision-making over traditional intuition-based management.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * Detail: (1) Risk mitigation and reduced cognitive bias, (2) Real-time operational efficiency and waste reduction, (3) Agility and rapid market response, and (4) Objective, measurable accountability across all tiers.

### #241
* **Question:** Demonstrate how a retail supermarket chain can apply descriptive and predictive analytics to manage inventory before a major festive season. State two specific data sources.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * **Data Sources:** Past festive POS transaction records and regional demographic search trends.
  * **Descriptive:** Analyzes historical stockout patterns and SKU sales velocity.
  * **Predictive:** Deploys time-series models (e.g., ARIMA) to forecast weekly stock requirements per store, automating supplier replenishment.

### #242
* **Question:** Apply the concept of data-driven decision-making to a ride-sharing platform (such as Uber or Ola). Show how real-time driver locations, customer demand heatmaps, and traffic data determine dynamic pricing.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * **Data Inputs:** GPS driver telematics, customer app request pings, live traffic APIs.
  * **Execution:** Algorithmic detection of localized supply-demand imbalance calculates real-time surge pricing, filtering price-sensitive demand while drawing peripheral driver supply to the zone.

### #243
* **Question:** Explain the strategic scope of business analytics in modern organizations. Discuss how converting raw transactional data into actionable business intelligence supports long-term strategic positioning.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * Discuss the transformation lifecycle: Raw Data Ingestion $\rightarrow$ Processing & Feature Engineering $\rightarrow$ Statistical Intelligence $\rightarrow$ Strategic Differentiation.
  * Explain moving from reactive hindsight to predictive and prescriptive strategic positioning.

### #244
* **Question:** Examine the transition of an organization moving from an intuition-led culture to a data-driven culture. Analyze the major technical, operational, and behavioral challenges managers face.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * **Technical:** Siloed legacy databases, poor data hygiene/governance, lack of cloud infrastructure.
  * **Operational:** Skill gaps between data engineers and business managers, process inertia.
  * **Behavioral:** Executive ego ("gut feel" supremacy), confirmation bias in metric selection, and employee fear of automated KPI monitoring.
