## Long Answer Questions (5 Marks)

### #245
* **Question:** Analyze a career dilemma between a stable corporate job and a small entrepreneurial business using a complete SWOT Analysis framework.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * **Job SWOT:** Strengths (predictable salary, stability, corporate pedigree), Weaknesses (limited autonomy, bureaucracy), Opportunities (vertical promotions), Threats (industry layoffs, automation).
  * **Business SWOT:** Strengths (complete independence, equity upside), Weaknesses (undercapitalized, lack of support structure), Opportunities (unlimited market scalability), Threats (cash burn, bankruptcy).
  * **Synthesis:** Recommend based on risk tolerance profile.

### #246
* **Question:** A clothing store notices high first-time customer drop-off. Analyze how the store can use CRM analytics across data collection, personalization, service recovery, and loyalty programs to retain customers.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * **Data Tracking:** Ingest purchase history, size preferences, and cart abandonment data.
  * **Segmented Personalization:** Send automated replenishment reminders and style suggestions matching prior purchases.
  * **Service Recovery:** Rapid automated ticketing for returns and negative feedback.
  * **Loyalty Program:** Tiered loyalty points rewarding second and third repeat visits.

### #247
* **Question:** Evaluate a supply chain dilemma: keep an existing low-cost supplier with frequent delays or switch to an expensive, reliable supplier.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * Evaluate Total Cost of Ownership (TCO): Factor in hidden costs of late deliveries (idle factory time, lost customer sales, SLA cancellation penalties).
  * Conclude that supplier reliability and on-time delivery often deliver superior net economic value despite higher nominal unit price.

### #248
* **Question:** Evaluate how an HR department facing declining productivity and rising turnover can use HR analytics to diagnose causes and take data-driven action.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * **Diagnostics:** Correlate turnover against tenure, manager feedback ratings, compensation ratios, and training completion rates.
  * **Interventions:** Identify high-risk flight cohorts, restructure toxic managerial teams, and align compensation with market benchmarks.

### #249
* **Question:** Define CRM data and explain how CRM analytics helps a real-world enterprise like Amazon use customer browsing and purchase data to personalize experiences and build loyalty.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * **Definition:** Comprehensive repository of customer transactional, behavioural, and communication records.
  * **Amazon Case Application:** Collaborative filtering ("Customers who bought this also bought..."), personalized homepage dynamic feeds, and Prime delivery loyalty retention.

### #253
* **Question:** Define supply chain analytics and CRM analytics in the context of an enterprise, and state one key purpose of each.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * **Supply Chain Analytics:** Tracking supplier performance, inventory turns, logistics latency; Purpose: Minimize operational cost and delivery friction.
  * **CRM Analytics:** Evaluating customer acquisition, retention, and lifetime value; Purpose: Maximize customer lifetime value (LTV) and loyalty.

### #254
* **Question:** Analyze how a company can integrate supply chain analytics and CRM analytics to improve its overall business performance.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * Connect demand-side insights (CRM customer demand forecasts) directly with supply-side planning (supply chain raw material procurement and warehouse safety stock), eliminating both stockouts and excess inventory write-downs.

### #255
* **Question:** Analyze how a business can use data-driven management across SWOT analysis, CRM, HR analytics, and supply chain analytics to support long-term success.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * Show cross-functional integration: SWOT sets strategic vision, CRM maximizes customer revenue, Supply Chain minimizes fulfillment expense, and HR analytics optimizes employee productivity.

### #256
* **Question:** Evaluate the benefits and limitations of combining SWOT analysis, CRM analytics, HR analytics, and supply chain analytics across an enterprise.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * **Benefits:** 360-degree operational visibility, objective cross-functional resource allocation, faster market responsiveness.
  * **Limitations:** High data infrastructure costs, risk of metric misalignment, data silos, and executive cognitive bias.

### #257
* **Question:** Evaluate how a business can use data and analytics to improve decision-making across customer management, employee performance, and inventory logistics.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * Detail concrete KPI feedback loops: Customer NPS/LTV (CRM), Employee retention/productivity (HR Analytics), and Inventory turn ratio/Order lead time (Supply Chain).

### #258
* **Question:** Evaluate how managers can improve the quality of business decisions by combining analytical tools with careful judgment. Discuss the importance of reducing bias while using data.
* **Answer Framework:**
  * Emphasize that data alone is insufficient if filtered through cognitive bias (e.g., confirmation bias or cherry-picking).
  * Conclude that optimal decision quality requires objective empirical analytics paired with transparent, debiased managerial scrutiny.
